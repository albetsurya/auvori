## 1. Why Phase 6 License Claims Needed Hardening

In Phase 6, `tests/tools/download_sample_photos.py` and `tests/run_p6_visual_quality_tests.py` trusted the manifest `license_name` string and `verification_status: "verified-original"` without verifying independent archive evidence on each Wikimedia Commons file page.

Because all 50 photos originate from **Unsplash**, and Unsplash switched from **CC0 1.0 Universal** to the non-CC0 **Unsplash License** on **June 5, 2017**, a Wikimedia Commons file tagged `{{Unsplash}}` requires verifiable proof of pre-June-5-2017 CC0 publication:
- Empirical check on `https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000id_/https://unsplash.com/license` confirmed explicit wording: *"All photos published on Unsplash are licensed under Creative Commons Zero..."*
- Empirical check on `https://web.archive.org/web/20170623000000id_/https://unsplash.com/license` confirmed that by 2017-06-23 Unsplash had already replaced CC0 with *"Unsplash grants you a nonexclusive copyright license..."*.
